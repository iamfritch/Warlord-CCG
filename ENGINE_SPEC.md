Warlord: Saga of the Storm (v2.0.8) — C# Engine Specification1. Core State, Zones \& Win ConditionsGolden Rule (103.1): Card text always overrides comprehensive rules.   General vs. Specific (702.1): If two effects conflict, the more specific effect OR the one imposing a negative/restrictive condition wins. If tied, earliest timestamp wins.   Victory/Loss (101.1, 403.4): A player immediately loses when they control 0 characters with the Warlord character type. Running out of cards in a deck does not cause a loss and the discard pile is not reshuffled; the player simply cannot draw.   Zones (400):In-Play Zones: Formation (Ranks 1..N per player), ActionZone (temporary resolution zone).   Out-of-Play Zones: Deck (ordered, hidden), Hand (hidden, no max size, default draw target = 5), DiscardPile (ordered, public), BanishZone (public, isolated).   Army vs. Formation (102.3): Army = all cards controlled by a player in play. Formation = characters/items in that player's ranks (excludes characters currently in an opponent's formation via e.g. Stealth).   Leaving Play Cleanup (104.8, 400.3, 700.5, 704.3, 714.2): When a card leaves play:Remove all wounds, counters, and ongoing effects on it.   Return control to its Owner and send it to its Owner's destination zone.   Any equipped items are destroyed and attached cards are discarded to their owners' discard piles one at a time in owner's chosen order: (1) Equipped Items $\\rightarrow$ (2) Attached Actions $\\rightarrow$ (3) Other Attached Cards $\\rightarrow$ (4) The Character itself. (If the character was banished instead of killed, only the character goes to BanishZone; attachments still go to DiscardPile).   2. Deck Construction \& Pre-Game Setup (108, 500)Deck Rules (108): Min 50 cards (includes starting army). Max 3 copies of any card by name (Max 1 if Epic keyword). Max 50% of total deck size for any single card type (Character, Item, Action). Max 1 Warlord (and max 1 Overlord, Daemonlord, Dragon Lord, Medusan Lord).   Starting Army (500.2-500.4): Revealed simultaneously before Turn 1:   Rank 1 (Front): Three Level 1 characters.   Rank 2: Two Level 2 characters.   Rank 3: One Warlord character.   Constraints: All 5 non-Warlord characters must share a faction with the Warlord, cannot have the Reserved keyword (602.3), and cannot include duplicate Unique cards (602.7).   Pre-Game Static Effects (500.5): No actions allowed before Turn 1. If both players have pre-game static effects, players make an unmodified, un-interactable d20 roll-off (reroll ties) and alternate resolving one effect at a time starting with the winner.   3. Card Anatomy, Stats \& OrientationOrientations (107):Ready (0° upright): Can be spent or stunned.   Spent (90°): Cannot be spent again (107.3). Can still perform non-Spend Orders/Reacts (510.6).   Stunned (180°): Also counts as Spent (107.4.2). Cannot be stunned again (107.4.1). Stunned characters cannot perform ANY actions, including actions on their equipped items (107.4.3). Stunned items grant passive stats/effects, but their printed actions cannot be performed (107.4.4).   State Transitions: Ready(card) sets Spent or Stunned $\\rightarrow$ Ready (107.4.5). Unstun(card) sets Stunned $\\rightarrow$ Spent (107.4.6).   Stats (201-211): ATK (array of strike bonuses, e.g., +3/+1 = 2 strikes), AC, Level, Skill, HP.   Numerals (705): Refers to absolute value/magnitude ignoring +/- sign. Increasing a numeral on -2 AC by 1 makes it -3 AC. Ordinal rank numbers (1st, 2nd) are not numerals.   Classes \& Factions (203, 205, 301.3): Multi-class or Multi-faction cards count as all of their classes/factions simultaneously. Sharing at least one faction/alignment means they are the "same" faction/alignment (and never "different" or "opposite").   Spells (303.6): All Cleric and Wizard Action cards are Spells. Non-card actions/strikes made into spells by an effect are Level 0 (unmodifiable).   4. Formation Geometry \& Illegal Ranks (401, 710)Rank Adjacency \& Distance (401.10-15):Player 1's Rank 1 and Player 2's Rank 1 are adjacent (distance = 1 rank away).   Same Rank: Characters directly next to each other (left/right) in the same rank are Adjacent. Leftmost and rightmost characters (including a solo character) are at the End of the rank (401.13).   "Within X Ranks" (401.15): Includes the source's current rank (0), plus up to X ranks forward (crossing into opposing formation) and X ranks backward.   Movement vs. Switching (401.5-17):Moving Forward = toward Rank 1. Moving Backward = toward higher rank numbers. Characters in their own Rank 1 cannot move forward.   Multi-rank moves jump directly to the destination rank without passing through intermediate ranks (401.9).   Switch (401.12), Replace (401.16), and Rearrange (401.17) do not count as movement and do not trigger movement effects or illegal ranks.   Illegal Ranks \& Falling Forward (401.6) \[STATE-BASED INTERRUPT]:Rule: Rank $R$ ($R > 1$) is illegal if $\\text{Count}(\\text{Rank } R) > \\text{Count}(\\text{Rank } R-1)$. (Items (302.2) and opposing characters in your formation (710.5) do not count toward rank character counts).   Resolution: Immediately interrupts play (even mid-action) before any other step or React:   If both players have illegal ranks, Initiative winner fixes all of theirs first (401.6.7).   Controller chooses one character at a time from an illegal rank to Fall Forward (R $\\rightarrow$ R-1) until all ranks are legal.   Orientation Penalty (401.6.3): Ready $\\rightarrow$ Spent + move forward 1; Spent $\\rightarrow$ Stunned + move forward 1; Stunned $\\rightarrow$ remains Stunned + move forward 1.   Counts as Moving, does not count as Maneuvering or a card effect (401.6.4-5).   Exception (401.6.1.1): If a character falling forward triggers a React, resolve that React before fixing remaining illegal ranks.   In Another Formation (710):Characters can only enter an opposing formation via explicit effects (e.g., Stealth into opposing Rank 1).   If a character in an opposing formation is ever behind that formation's last occupied rank, immediately snap them to the last occupied rank (not movement).   If either formation has 0 characters controlled by its owner, all characters in foreign formations immediately snap back to their own Rank 1 (not movement).   While in an opposing formation, a character does not add their rank number to their level when equipping items (710.8).   5. Wounds, Dying \& State-Based Death (104)Death Check (104.2, 104.6): Whenever a character's Wounds >= Total HP, Total HP <= 0, or Actual Level <= 0 (note: action-specific level penalties do not kill, 510.13.2), the character is marked IsDying = true.   Single Death Trigger Rule (104.2.1, 104.9): Once IsDying is set, open the "Before this character dies / Death React" window.   Taking additional wounds while IsDying does not trigger a second death window.   CRITICAL (104.9): Removing/healing wounds during the Death React window so that Wounds < HP DOES NOT SAVE THE CHARACTER. Once determined to be dying, death can only be prevented by an effect that explicitly states the character "no longer dies / does not die", or by moving/attaching the character to another zone/card (508.7, 711.3).   Banish Replacement (104.2.2): If an effect says "banish instead of killed", the character does not die, and no Death/Kill Reacts trigger.   Self-Death Reacts (104.12): Printed text on a character saying "After this character is killed" is treated as "Before this character is killed".   6. Dice, Checks, Saves \& Modifiers (105, 106)d20 Rolls (105.1):Natural 20 (Critical Success): Automatic success regardless of DC/AC or modifiers. Cannot be rerolled or changed by any effect (105.1.4). (Engine Note: In Strikes, a Crit Success simply guarantees a hit and inflicts the normal 1 wound unless a card effect specifies otherwise—it does NOT automatically deal 2 wounds).   Natural 1 (Critical Failure): Automatic failure regardless of DC/AC or modifiers. Cannot be rerolled or changed by any effect (105.1.4).   Advantage \& Disadvantage (105.2):Advantage: Roll 2d20, keep higher. Disadvantage: Roll 2d20, keep lower.   Multiple instances do not stack (max 2 dice rolled).   If a roll has both Advantage and Disadvantage (regardless of counts), they completely cancel out $\\rightarrow$ roll 1d20 (105.2.4).   Planar Exception (105.2.3): When striking a Planar character (with a non-spell strike), Advantage keeps the highest even roll (if any); Disadvantage keeps the lowest odd roll (if any).   Rerolls (105.5): Rerolling an Advantage/Disadvantage roll requires rerolling all dice.   Preset Rolls (105.3): Do not roll dice; cannot be changed by rerolls or Advantage/Disadvantage.   Skill Checks \& Saves (106):Formula: d20 + Character.Skill + SpecificModifiers >= DC.   Hierarchy (106.2): All Saves are Skill Checks, but not all Skill Checks are Saves (bonuses to Skill Checks apply to Saves; bonuses to Saves do not apply to general Skill Checks). Players cannot voluntarily fail (106.3).   7. Turn Sequence (501–506)Ready Phase (502): Simultaneously for all players (including Turn 1): Spent cards $\\rightarrow$ Ready; Stunned cards $\\rightarrow$ Spent.   Draw Phase (503): Players may optionally discard any number of cards from hand. Then draw one card at a time until Hand.Count == HandSize (default 5). If Hand.Count >= HandSize, draw 0 (do not discard down).   Initiative Phase (504): Both Warlords roll 1d20 + InitiativeModifiers. Highest wins and acts first in all priority windows this turn. Nat 20 always beats non-20. Ties (including double Nat 20 or double Nat 1) reroll with the same modifiers carried over.   Order Phase (505): Starting with Initiative winner, players alternate taking one Order at a time (Play Character, Character Order, or Pass). Passing does not lock a player out if the opponent takes an Order afterward. Phase ends when both players Pass consecutively (505.1). (Pass is not an Order or Action, 505.5).   End of Turn Phase (506):Step 1: Alternating in Initiative order, players may play/perform "at the end of turn" actions/cards.   Step 2: Alternating in Initiative order, expire "until end of turn" effects one at a time (if an effect applies to multiple cards, its creator chooses the individual card expiration order).   8. Orders \& Equipping Rules (302, 507)A player on their turn in the Order Phase may do one of:Play a Character from Hand (507.1): Performed by the player.   Rank Placement: Must be placed in Rank == Character.EffectiveEnteringLevel (min Rank 1).   Rank Legality Check (507.1.2-3): Cannot be played if it creates an illegal rank (unless an immediate React/effect makes it legal). Even if an effect allows entering a specific/arbitrary rank, Rank $R-1$ must be legal and occupied.   Loyalty Penalty (507.1.4): If the character does not share a faction with your Warlord AND does not have the Mercenary Guilds faction, it enters play Stunned.   Warlord Rule (507.1.6): Any character with the Warlord type entering play during the game loses the Warlord type.   Standard Order — Perform an Attack (507.3): Spend Order: Perform an attack.   Spend the ready character. Perform consecutive melee strikes targeting opposing characters (507.3.4).   Order of Strikes: Printed strikes must be performed first in left-to-right order (507.3.3), followed by extra strikes gained from items/effects in any order.   Stopping Early (507.3.5): The 1st strike is mandatory. Any subsequent strikes (2nd, 3rd, etc.) are optional and may be skipped before declaring them.   Standard Order — Equip an Item from Hand (302, 507.4): Order: Reveal an Item card from your hand: Equip the item to this character. (Does NOT spend the character!)   Requirements:Character is Ready (not Stunned, 107.4.3) and does not have Unarmed (602.15).   Character matches at least one required Class and any required Traits (302.3).   Level Check (302.4, 710.8):$$\\text{Character.EffectiveEquipLevel} + (\\text{InOwnFormation} \\ ? \\ \\text{CurrentRank} : 0) \\ge \\text{Item.Level}$$   Character does not already have an item with the exact same Card Name equipped (302.5).   Character does not have a Cursed item equipped in the same Equip Location (602.2.1).   Equip Locations \& State-Based Destruction (302.7):Locations: Armor, Arrow, Bow, Familiar, Gauntlet, Helm, Shield, Stance, Steed, Trap, Weapon.   Max 1 item per Equip Location (unless modified by e.g. Dual Wield 602.12: allows 2 Weapons, or 1 Weapon + 1 Shield, never 2 Weapons + 1 Shield).   Interrupt: Immediately upon equipping (before Reacts to equipping trigger), if a location limit is exceeded, the controller chooses and destroys excess equipped items in that location (may choose the newly equipped item). Static abilities on the newly equipped item (like granting Dual Wield or losing the Weapon trait) apply before checking for destruction (302.7.3).   Standard Order — Maneuver (507.5): Spend Order: Move this character forward 1 rank, backward 1 rank, or to a different position in their current rank. Immediately check/fix illegal ranks (401.6).   Action Card / In-Play Order (507.6-7): Select a non-stunned character you control that meets Class, Trait, and Level (Character.EffectiveActionLevel >= ActionCard.Level) requirements. Note: Actions on equipped items can be performed even if the item is Spent (unless the item action requires spending the item), provided the item is not Stunned (507.7.2).   9. Actions, Reacts \& Timing Windows (508, 510)Syntax \& Costs (510.2): \[Timing/Cost Prefix]: \[Additional Cost]: \[Effect].Spend Order: / Spend React: = Order/React: Spend this character:. If "Spend" is not in the cost, a Spent character can perform the Order/React (510.6)!   Destroy Order/React: = must destroy the item as part of the cost (510.20).   Roll Order/React: = roll d20 as part of the cost (510.19).   Limited (or "Once per turn"): Can only be performed once per turn per ability instance (510.7). Losing and regaining a conditional Limited ability in the same turn does not reset its limit (510.7.1), unless the card leaves play and re-enters play (510.21).   Ownership of Costs (510.2): Costs (killing, spending, wounding, removing counters) can only be paid using cards/counters you control.   Illegal Actions \& Zero Effect (510.9):An action cannot be declared if costs/requirements/valid targets cannot be met (unless an immediate self-React/effect makes it legal, 510.9.1).   Zero Effect (510.9.2): An action is illegal and cannot be declared if its effect would produce no actual change to the game state beyond paying its cost. (Choosing 0 on an "up to X" effect is legal, 510.9.4).   Action Resolution Lifecycle (510.5):Declare Action \& Performer: Check restrictions. $\\rightarrow$ Window: Before Performing / Before Casting.   Pay Costs (includes cost-targeting):Window: Before Paying Cost   Pay all costs. (If cost is prevented, action is canceled; if cost is undone after payment, e.g., readying a spent character, cost remains paid).   Window: After Paying Cost   Generate Effects (includes effect-targeting): Resolve effects in printed sentence order (510.3.2). Do as much as possible (510.3). Lock in dynamic stat variables (X) when generated (510.4). (Reacts to an effect happen here, 508.12).   Resolve Action: Move Action card from ActionZone to DiscardPile (unless attached/banished/returned). $\\rightarrow$ Window: After Performing / After Casting.   Cancellation Rules (510.10, 510.12):If an action/strike is canceled, or if the performing character leaves play via an external React/strike before completion (510.12), stop immediately. Costs remain paid, Limited use is consumed, and any character/item card being put into play/equipped is discarded. (If the item or action card leaves play after costs are paid, the action still resolves, 510.12).   React System (508):Once Per Trigger (508.2): A specific card/ability instance can only execute once per single trigger event (a second copy of the same card in hand may be used for the same trigger).   Priority Order (508.3, 512.3): Initiative winner has first opportunity to React (or pass), then opponent. After a React resolves, if the trigger condition is still valid, priority passes to the other player (a player must let the opponent respond between multiple Reacts to the same trigger). Actions (Reacts) always resolve before triggered Ongoing/Static effects on the same event (512.3).   Presence \& Range Requirement (508.10): A character must be in play and within any required rank distance at the exact moment the trigger occurs to React to it. (e.g., If a Rank 1 death causes a Rank 2 character to fall forward into Rank 1, that character was not in Rank 1 when the death occurred and cannot use an "After a character in this rank dies" React).   Named React Shorthands (508.6):Death React: = "React: Before this character is killed:" (while still in play).   Kill React: = "React: After this character kills target character:" (after victim hits discard pile; targets the dead card; uses victim's stats at moment of death 508.9).   Enter React: = "React: After this character enters play:".   "Using" vs. "Performing" (510.26): "Using an action" = playing an Action Card. "Performing an action" = any Order or React (cards, items, character text, feats).   10. Strikes \& Combat Resolution (509)Strike Types: Strikes are effects, not independent actions (509.2).   Melee Strike (509.3): Default range = within 1 rank (same rank, adjacent rank, or opposing Rank 1 from your Rank 1; Reach keyword extends to 2 ranks 602.16). Roll d20 + BaseATK + ATKModifiers >= Target.AC.   Ranged Strike (509.4): Default range = exactly 2 ranks away (cannot target 0 or 1 rank away!). Character's printed ATK stat and melee strike count do not apply to ranged strikes (509.4.1). Roll d20 + RangedStrikeBonus + RangedModifiers >= Target.AC.   Explicit Target Range Override (509.3.3, 509.4.2): If an effect instructs a character to perform a melee or ranged strike against a specified character (e.g., "strike that character" or Riposte), ignore range restrictions.   7 Steps of a Strike (509.5):Declare Strike \& Performer $\\rightarrow$ Window: Before Performing Strike   Choose Target:Window: Before Targeting (e.g., Marksmanship feat triggers here!)   Select legal target.   Window: After Targeting (e.g., Defend, Stealth feats trigger here!)   Make Strike Roll:Window: Before Rolling (e.g., Powerattack, Charisma feats trigger here!)   Roll d20 (apply Advantage/Disadvantage/Planar rules).   Window: After Rolling   Determine Hit or Miss (RollTotal >= Target.AC, Nat 20, Nat 1, Planar odd miss):Window: Before Hitting / Before Missing   Lock in Hit/Miss.   Window: After Hitting / If a strike hits / After Missing   Determine Wounds (If Hit): Default = 1 wound. (If a strike is set to "inflict 0/no wounds", bonus wound modifiers cannot increase it above 0, 509.10).   Inflict Wounds (If Hit):Window: Before Wounds   Apply wounds (or "instead of wounds" replacement effects) $\\rightarrow$ Run Death Check (104.2).   Window: After Wounds   Resolve Strike $\\rightarrow$ Window: After Performing Strike (e.g., Riposte triggers here!)   11. Targeting \& Redirection (511)What Targets (511.1-4): Only actions and strikes target, and only while being performed. Static effects never target (unless producing a strike). An action targets a card in play (or discard/banish if specified) even without the word "target" if it modifies stats/attributes/zone/orientation, forces a save/check, grants abilities, restricts actions/movement, cancels/redirects its action, heals/wounds/kills it, modifies its die rolls, or targets/destroys/attaches to its equipped/attached cards (511.2.1-12).   Mass / Multi-Targeting (511.5): Resolved one target at a time in controller's chosen order. Characters that cannot be targeted (e.g., Ward) or would yield Zero Effect are skipped; the action is legal as long as $\\ge 1$ valid target exists.   Redirection (511.6): Changing a strike/action's target requires the new target to be a legal original target (must satisfy range, orientation, and targeting restrictions from the performer's perspective). Once redirected away from Character A, Character A is treated as never having been targeted.   12. Durations, Tokens, Counters \& Special Card States (700–714)Default Durations (700.3-4): Stat and Feat bonuses/penalties generated by actions last until end of turn unless stated otherwise (e.g., "permanently" = while card remains in play, 512.4, 700.5).   Location vs. Entity Buffs (700.8):"All characters in your 1st rank gain +X" = Zone aura (applies dynamically to whoever enters/leaves Rank 1).   "Adjacent characters gain +X" or "Target character gains +X" = Locked entity buff (locks onto those specific characters at resolution and stays on them even if they move).   Deck Searching/Shuffling (701.1): Any search/retrieve from the deck requires shuffling afterward, unless only looking at/revealing the top $N$ cards and placing them in defined zones (hand/top/bottom/discard).   Standard Tokens (703.9): Cease to exist after leaving play (though they still trigger "enters discard/banish" events first, 703.5).   Trap Token: Level 1 Classless Item (Trap equip location). Text: "Destroy React: After target character targets this character with a strike: The target must succeed with a reflex save (DC 10) or suffer a wound."   Simple Shield Token: Level 1 Classless Shield Item. Text: "Destroy React: Before this character suffers wounds: Reduce the wounds by one."   Animal Token: Level 2 Good Classless Mercenary Guilds Character, Beast trait, +2 Physical ATK, 8 AC, +0 Skill, 1 HP.   Stat Counters (704.6): Named counters (ATK, AC, Skill, Level, HP counters) grant +1 to that stat per counter.   Considered Blank (713): Loses all game stats, attributes (traits, keywords, classes, factions, alignments, damage types), and textbox abilities. Retains only Card Name and Card Type(s), and remains equipped/attached.
